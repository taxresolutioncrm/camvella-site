FAQ

Questions landscape owners actually ask.

Can GroundIVO handle recurring lawn maintenance and project work?

Yes. The platform is designed for recurring route-based work as well as quoted projects, installs, repairs and one-time services.

Can multiple crews and offices use the same system?

Yes. GroundIVO supports office-aware data isolation, role-based access, employee workflows and multi-crew scheduling.

Does GroundIVO include estimating, scheduling and invoicing?

Yes. Those workflows share the same customer and property record so information does not need to be re-entered across separate tools.

Is there an employee portal?

Yes. GroundIVO includes employee time, assigned work and time-off workflows designed for field teams.
